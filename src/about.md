NextG Systems LLC is a New Jersey company building open, programmable, and
secure NextG wireless systems, with a focus on mission-critical
communications for defense and emergency services.

Our team has spent more than a decade on software-defined networking in the
data center and cloud, and several years building and operating private 5G
testbeds on open-source RAN and core software. We work closely with academic
collaborators, including Princeton University.

NextG Systems is a member of the National Spectrum Consortium.
